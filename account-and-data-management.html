Account & Data Management

Last updated: November 28, 2025

Manage your FinTrack account, request data deletion, or delete your account entirely. Choose the option that best fits your needs.

Request Full Account Deletion

This option will permanently delete your entire FinTrack account and all associated personal data. You will no longer be able to sign in or recover any data.

Data we collect

When you use FinTrack with Google Sign-In, we store the following personal data tied to your account:

  • Basic profile: full name, email address (from Google)
  • Account identifier: Firebase Authentication UID
  • User-generated financial data stored in Firestore: transactions, accounts, bills, categories, preferences
  • Crash reports and device info (Firebase Crashlytics)

If you want a complete list of data stored for your account, include your Google email in your deletion request.

What will be deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account record (name, email)
  • All personal financial data stored in Firestore under your user namespace: transactions, accounts, bills, categories, and preferences.

What may be retained

  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics data that cannot be tied back to you — used for improving the app's stability and performance.
  • Crash report logs: anonymized stack traces may persist for a short period to help identify and fix bugs.
  • Backups: copies of records may persist in backups for a short period; we will remove personal data from primary storage and backups will be purged according to our retention policy.

How to request account deletion

Send an email with your account details to verify and process your request:

Required information (copy & paste into your email):

Subject: Delete my FinTrack account

My full name:
My Google account email (used to sign in):
Date you last signed into the app (approx):
Any additional info to help locate my account:

I confirm I want my FinTrack account and associated personal data permanently deleted.

Verification & processing

  • We will verify that the request comes from the Google account owner — verification may include a reply from the same email address or asking for additional proof (screenshot of your account in the app).
  • After verification we will remove data tied to your account from our Firestore database and cancel any active notifications.
  • We will inform you by email when deletion is complete or if we need more information.
  • Deletion typically occurs within 7 business days of verification.

Suggested steps after deletion

  • Revoke app access in your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove FinTrack - Expense & Bill Manager.
  • Sign out of the app on your devices and uninstall the app.
  • Delete any local data: uninstalling the app will remove locally-stored data from your device.

Request Deletion of Specific Data (Keep Account)

You can request deletion of specific categories of data without deleting your entire account. Your account and authentication will remain active.

What data can be deleted

You can request deletion of any of the following without affecting your account:

  • All transactions: Delete all income and expense entries while keeping accounts and categories.
  • All bills: Delete all bill records and associated reminders.
  • Specific transactions: Request deletion of transactions from a specific date range or category.
  • All accounts: Delete all account records (this will also affect associated transactions).

If you need to delete data not listed here, describe it in your request and we will review it.

What remains

  • Your account and authentication credentials (you can still sign in).
  • Any data you did not request to be deleted.
  • Your basic profile information (name, email) — remains tied to your account.

Important: Be specific about which data you want deleted. For example, if you want to delete transactions from a specific date range, include the start and end dates.

Verification & processing

  • We will verify that the request comes from the account owner — verification may include a reply from the same email address.
  • After verification we will delete only the data you requested from Firestore.
  • We will inform you by email when deletion is complete, with a summary of what was deleted.
  • Deletion typically occurs within 7 business days of verification.
  • You can continue using the app normally after deletion of specific data.

Can I undo this?

No, data deletion is permanent.

General information

  • Timeline: Requests are typically processed within 7 business days of verification.
  • Backups: Copies of records may persist in backups for a short period; we will remove data from primary storage and backups will be purged according to our retention policy.
  • Questions: If you have any questions about the deletion process or need help locating your account, contact us at tauhedul.amin@gmail.com.
