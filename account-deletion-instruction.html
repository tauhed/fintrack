Request Account & Data Deletion — FinTrack

Use this page to request deletion of your account and any personal data associated with FinTrack - Expense & Bill Manager (developer: M. Tauhedul Amin).

What this page is for

This page describes how to request deletion of your FinTrack account and what will happen after we receive your request. Please read the instructions carefully and provide the information requested so we can verify your request and remove your data.

Data we collect (examples)

The following are personal data types we may have tied to your account via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication:

  • Basic profile: full name, email address (from Google)
  • Account identifier: Firebase Authentication UID
  • User-generated financial data stored in Firestore: transactions, accounts, bills, categories, preferences
  • Crash reports and device info (Firebase Crashlytics)

If you want a complete list of data stored for your account, include your Google email and Firebase UID in your deletion request (see the "How to request" section).

What will be deleted

  • All personal financial data stored in Firestore under your user namespace: transactions, accounts, bills, categories, and preferences.

What may be retained and why

  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics data that cannot be tied back to you — used for improving the app's stability and performance.
  • Crash report logs: anonymized stack traces may persist for a short period to help identify and fix bugs.
  • Backups: copies of records may persist in backups for a short period; we will remove personal data from primary storage and backups will be purged according to our retention policy.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to request deletion and include the required information so we can verify and process your request.

Email request

Send an email to: tauhedul.amin@gmail.com

Required information (copy & paste into your email):

Subject: Delete my FinTrack account

My full name:
My Google account email (used to sign in):
Date you last signed into the app (approx):
Any additional info to help locate my account:

I confirm I want my FinTrack account and associated personal data deleted. Please let me know if you require further verification.

How we verify and process your request

  • We will verify that the request comes from the Google account owner — verification may include a reply from the same email address
  • After verification we will remove data tied to your account from our Firestore database and cancel any active notifications.
  • We will inform you by email when deletion is complete or if we need more information.
  • Deletion typically occurs within 7 business days of verification.

Suggested steps you should take

  • Revoke app access in your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove FinTrack - Expense & Bill Manager.
  • Sign out of the app on your devices and uninstall the app if you no longer wish to use it.
  • Delete any local data: uninstalling the app will remove locally-stored data from your device.

Retention & timing

When we complete deletion from primary systems we will also schedule removal from backups according to our retention policy. If you require exact retention durations for specific data types, include your request in the email and we will provide details for your account.
