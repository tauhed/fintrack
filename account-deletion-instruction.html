What this page is for

This page describes how to request deletion of your account and what will happen after we receive your request. Please read the instructions carefully and provide the information requested so we can verify your request and remove your data.

Data we collect (examples)

The following are common personal data types we may have tied to your account via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication:

  • Basic profile: full name, email address, profile photo
  • Account identifier: Firebase Auth UID (internal ID)
  • User-generated content stored in Firestore / Realtime Database
  • Files stored in Firebase Storage (uploads, attachments)
  • Device and app usage info (analytics, crash reports)

If you want a complete list of data types for your particular account, include your Google email and Firebase UID in your deletion request (see the “How to request” section).

What will be deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account record (name, email, profile picture) — once deleted you will no longer be able to sign in with that account.
  • User data stored in our databases (e.g., Firestore documents) that are directly linked to your account.
  • Files in Firebase Storage that were uploaded by you.
  • Push notification tokens associated with your account.
  • Any other personal data we maintain that can be tied to your account.

What may be retained and why

  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics data that cannot be tied back to an individual — used for improving the app.
  • Information we are required to keep for legal, tax, or accounting reasons (for example, transaction receipts) — only the minimum required and kept only as long as legally necessary.
  • Backups and logs: copies of some records may persist in backups for a short period; we will remove personal data from primary storage and schedules will remove it from backups according to our retention policy.

How we verify and process your request

  • We will verify that the request comes from the Google account owner — verification may include a reply from the same email address or asking for additional proof (screenshot showing you signed in).
  • After verification we will remove data tied to the account from our production databases and storage.
  • We will inform you by email when deletion is complete or if we need more information.

Suggested steps you should take

  • Revoke app access in your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove YOUR_APP_NAME.
  • Sign out of the app on your devices and uninstall the app if you no longer wish to use it.
  • If you use the same Google account for other services, consider whether you want to keep or update those service settings separately.

Retention & timing

When we complete deletion from primary systems we will also schedule removal from backups according to our retention policy. Certain records required for legal reasons may be retained for a limited period. If you require exact retention durations for specific data types, include your request in the message email and we will provide details for your account.
