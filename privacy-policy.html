Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how M. Tauhedul Amin ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the mobile application FinTrack - Expense & Bill Manager ("the app"). By using the app you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account & Authentication

When you sign in with Google Sign-In we collect basic account information provided by Google such as your name and email address. This information is used to create and manage your account in the app.

1.2 User-Provided Financial Data

You manually enter information into the app such as:

  • Income entries
  • Expense entries
  • Bill details and reminder dates
  • Account names and categories

The app does not access your bank accounts, cards, or external financial institutions — only the data you enter is stored.

1.3 Device & Usage Data

We use Google Firebase services to improve app reliability and performance. Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects crash reports to help us identify and fix bugs. This data includes stack traces and device information but no personal financial data.

1.4 Local Storage

Local data is stored on your device using local storage. Guest data uses the key "guest"; signed-in user data synced to firestore immediate after login.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Account creation and authentication (Google Sign-In).
  • Syncing your financial data across devices using Firebase Firestore.
  • Scheduling and sending bill reminders (local notifications).
  • Improving app stability via crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics).

3. Data Storage & Security

Cloud-synced data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore under your user space. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). We use Firebase services (Firestore, Authentication, Crashlytics). We follow reasonable security practices but cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We use Google Firebase as a service provider to operate the app's backend and crash reporting. Firebase processes data only to the extent necessary to provide these services and is contractually required to protect data according to Google's Firebase Privacy.

5. Notifications

The app schedules local bill reminder notifications on your device. No data is sent to external services for notifications.

6. Advertising

The app does not currently display advertising.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and user-entered data while your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by:

  • Contacting us at tauhedul.amin@gmail.com with your request.

After confirming your identity and request, we will delete your data within a reasonable time frame and confirm deletion by email.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in data centers located outside your country (for example Google-managed data centers). By using the app you consent to the transfer of information to locations outside your country.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. When we make material changes, we will revise the “Last updated” date and notify users if required by law.
