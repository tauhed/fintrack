Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how M. Tauhedul Amin ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the mobile application FinTrack - Expense & Bill Manager ("the app"). By using the app you consent to the collection and use of information described here.

Information We Collect

Account & Authentication

If you sign in with Google, we collect basic account information provided by Google such as your name and email address to create and manage your account.

User-Provided Financial Data

The app stores information you enter manually, including:

  • Income and expense entries
  • Bill details and reminder dates
  • Account names, balances and categories

The app does not access your bank accounts or cards. Only the information you enter is stored.

Device & Usage Data

We use Google Firebase services to improve app reliability and performance. Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects crash reports to help us identify and fix bugs. This data includes stack traces and device information but no personal financial data.

Local Storage

Local data is stored on your device using local storage. Guest data uses the key "guest"; signed-in user data uses a key that includes your user ID (for example transactions_<userId> and preferences_<userId>).

How We Use Your Information

  • Account creation and authentication (Google Sign-In).
  • Syncing your financial data across devices using Firebase Firestore.
  • Scheduling and sending bill reminders (local notifications).
  • Improving app stability via crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics).

Data Storage & Security

Cloud-synced data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore under your user space. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). We use Firebase services (Firestore, Authentication, Crashlytics). We follow reasonable security practices but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third Parties

We do not sell or rent personal data. We use Google Firebase as a service provider to operate the app's backend and crash reporting. Firebase processes data only to the extent necessary to provide these services and is contractually required to protect data according to Google's Firebase Privacy.

Notifications

The app schedules local bill reminder notifications on your device. No data is sent to external services for notifications.

Advertising

The app does not currently display advertising.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain account and user-entered data while your account exists. You can request deletion by:

  • Using the in-app "Delete account" option if available, or
  • Contacting us at tauhedul.amin@gmail.com with your request.

After verifying the request, we will delete your data and confirm by email. Local guest data remains on the device unless removed by the user or migrated during sign-in.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, contact us to request deletion.

International Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in data centers located outside your country (for example Google-managed data centers). By using the app you consent to cross-border data transfers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When material changes occur we will update the "Last updated" date. For significant changes we will notify users where reasonably possible.
